The offer

A prototype, offered — nothing more, nothing assumed

The AGI Future Foundation built a working VR training prototype inspired by the Unspoken Smiles Foundation's public work, and offers it here, in the open: try it, tell us what is wrong, and decide — on your terms, under a written agreement — whether it earns a pilot. This page is the whole of the offer. Publishing it is not a partnership, and no reply is owed.

The framing, in full. This is a demo program inspired by Unspoken Smiles' public work. It is not an official Unspoken Smiles program, not endorsed by or affiliated with Unspoken Smiles. No partnership, pilot, or contact is claimed — none exists; no approach has been made beyond publishing this offer. The demo does not license or certify anyone, makes no clinical claims, and holds no patient data. Unspoken Smiles' name is used on this site in text only, as the subject of this offer; their branding and logo are not used.

What is being offered

1 · The prototype

A working demo, not a mockup

A browser-based 3D dental clinic: one fully playable session (Prevention & School Screening), chairside and sterilisation training, two labs, a simulation studio, and a Fellows' learning library — all sourced, all inspectable.

Open the live demo · Read the code

2 · The ask

Review first, pilot only on evidence

A practising dental assistant or hygienist review of the clinical sequences, specs and language. ULACIT faculty review, if they choose — an invitation, not an assumed partnership. Then, if the review supports it, a small pilot cohort of Fellows, coach-supervised, with feedback loops built in.

3 · The condition

Written agreement before any use

Nothing of theirs is used on goodwill: no name, logo, or learners in any real programme until a written agreement exists. If the answer is no — or no answer — the prototype stays what it is: a public demo that makes no claim on them.

The roadmap the offer proposes

PhaseFocus
Phase 0Prototype: browser clinic, screening session, labs, and wiki — the current build.
Phase 1Expert review and correction: practising DA / RDH review of sequences, specs, and language.
Phase 2Learning Library depth: full Fellows paths, printable packs, Spanish narration parity.
Phase 3Small pilot cohort with a coach dashboard and structured feedback.
Phase 4Real WebXR session: implement, then test on Quest hardware before any claim is made.
Phase 5Localisation and site adaptation for Costa Rica and Americas cohorts.
Phase 6Scale only on pilot evidence: more stations, more cases, partner sites under written agreement.

Phases 1–6 are proposals inside this offer. Only Phase 0 exists today.

About Unspoken Smiles — public facts, kept separate

The facts below are about the real foundation, taken from its own public materials. They describe their work — not this demo, and not any relationship with us.

  • The Unspoken Smiles Foundation (unspokensmiles.org) is a New York State 501(c)(3) founded by Jean Paul Laurent. Their materials give the founding as 2014; some sources give 2011 — we flag the conflict rather than pick one.
  • Their Zero Cavity Initiative is a prevention programme built around school-based screening and education; their page cites a CDC figure that about 20% of children have at least one decayed tooth, and their homepage states oral disease affects 3.58 billion people — nearly half the world's population.
  • Their Fellowship programme trains fellows who serve in Costa Rica and across the Americas; per the programme's published pages, ULACIT faculty supervise weekly at the foundation's San José clinic, with academic credit.

Sources: unspokensmiles.org (homepage, About, Zero Cavity and Fellowship pages), as recorded in this project's research notes, 2026-11-07.

What we are not claiming

  • Not an official Unspoken Smiles program; not endorsed by, affiliated with, or partnered with Unspoken Smiles.
  • No pilot is agreed, running, or implied. No contact has been made beyond publishing this offer.
  • No certification, licensure, or clinical claims: the demo does not license or certify anyone, and VR practice is not clinical hours.
  • No patient data: every case in the training is fictional, and findings are always given — learners never diagnose.
  • No use of Unspoken Smiles branding: their name appears in text only, as the subject of this offer.

If you are with the Unspoken Smiles Foundation: the door is x@agifuturefoundation.org (AGI Future Foundation). Try the demo first — it is the pitch.
