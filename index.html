What Perilux is

A training project built in the open by the AGI Future Foundation. Two builds carry it: the TradePath dental programme — full training sessions with stations, guided runs, and coach debriefs — and the Unspoken Smiles VR simulator, a playable browser clinic built as a demonstration and an offer.

The programme

Three core sessions

Prevention & School Screening · Chairside Assisting Basics · Sterilization & Operatory Setup — plus a supporting Dental Reception & Front Desk session. Each session runs the same way: lesson, checklist, tasks, debrief.

The simulator

Six stations, one clinic

Six training stations — PPE & hand hygiene, chairside, sterilisation, school screening, virtual patient, emergency drill — mapped with reference to CODA, DANB, CDC and ADEA competencies, and run on a seven-step, criterion-gated progression.

The VR clinic, in your browser

A 3D dental clinic with one fully playable session, two labs, a simulation studio and a learning library. No install, no account, no headset needed — it runs in a desktop or mobile browser view.

Built for the dental industry, in the open

The programme's library — tools, methods, and the real credential pathways — is published here with its sources, so practising dental assistants and hygienists can check every sequence and correct us. Specs appear only where verified against a primary source; anything unverified is marked TBD, never filled in.

Pathways

What a real credential requires

This training certifies nothing. It maps skills plainly to the external routes that do — DANB CDA, California RDA, CDC infection-control guidance — so learners can see the real road.
