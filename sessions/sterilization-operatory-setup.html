Session 3 · Sterilization

Sterilization & Operatory Setup

The session that keeps every other session safe. Instruments move in one direction only — dirty to clean to sterile, never backwards — through an eight-stage chain. In a graded run, a contamination event restarts the load: the chain teaches why order matters, not just what the order is.

Graded run · 8 stagesStation C of the simulatorSkills: instrument processing · operatory turnover · instrument tracking

Demo session. A demo program inspired by Unspoken Smiles' public work — not an official Unspoken Smiles program, not endorsed by or affiliated with Unspoken Smiles. The one-way chain — eight stages

  1. Pre-treat — hold and pre-treat used instruments so debris never dries on.
  2. Transport — move the load, contained, to the reprocessing area. One way only.
  3. Ultrasonic clean — the ultrasonic bath (35–48 kHz) cleans; it does not sterilise.
  4. Rinse — rinse the cleaned load.
  5. Inspect — every instrument under the magnifier. A damaged instrument that goes through the cycle is still a damaged instrument.
  6. Package — pouch, seal, label with the date, and log it before it goes to the autoclave.
  7. Autoclave — the sterilising cycle (Class B, 132–134 °C pre-vacuum in the simulator's spec), then verify the indicators.
  8. Store — sterile stock stored, labelled, and rotated — and chart it, or it did not happen: the sterilisation log is the record.

The standing rules: hand hygiene and PPE before touching dirty instruments · clean first, then sterilise · a pouch that is torn, wet, or unlabelled is not sterile — reprocess it · handpieces are heat-sterilised between every patient · dental unit waterlines are kept at or below 500 CFU/mL · a biological spore test runs at least weekly.

Grounded in the CDC Guidelines for Infection Control in Dental Health-Care Settings — 2003, MMWR 52(RR17): nine recommendation areas; ultrasonic and autoclave figures as recorded in the programme's verified research notes.

The guided run

  1. Hand hygiene + PPE before touching dirty instruments.
  2. Dirty to clean: rinse, then ultrasonic — one way only, never backwards.
  3. Inspect under the magnifier, pouch, seal, and label with the date.
  4. Autoclave cycle, then verify the indicators.
  5. Store sterile stock and run the turnover checklist with the coach.

Run steps from the TradePath Training Centre scenario data (sterilization zone). The fault drill in the VR clinic — the wet pack and the missed indicator — practises catching the two failures this session exists to prevent.
