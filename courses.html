Training courses

Please read first. The training programme on this site is a demo program inspired by Unspoken Smiles' public work. It is not an official Unspoken Smiles program, not endorsed by or affiliated with Unspoken Smiles. No partnership, pilot, or contact is claimed — none exists. The demo does not license or certify anyone, and it holds no patient data.

Every course below runs the same way: lesson → checklist → tasks → debrief, through a seven-step criterion-gated progression — a step unlocks when the work meets the criterion, not when the clock runs out. The sessions were built in the TradePath Training Centre. This is skills training and demonstration; simulation is not certification, and VR practice is not clinical hours.

The three core sessions

Session 1 · Prevention & School Screening

The flagship nine-step session on the screen → educate → refer workflow. Consent is checked before anyone is seen; the rule throughout is flag and refer, never diagnose.

Session 2 · Chairside Assisting Basics

The guided chairside run: four-handed dentistry — clock-face zones, single-handed transfer, grasps — and the patient comfort rule that governs every step.

Session 3 · Sterilization & Operatory Setup

The eight-stage one-way chain — pre-treat → transport → ultrasonic clean → rinse → inspect → package → autoclave → store — grounded in CDC guidance, practised until the order is automatic.

The front of the house

Dental Reception & Front Desk

The fourth strand of the programme: greeting, scheduling, records discipline, and the consent paperwork handled correctly — the chairside team's other half.

The VR simulator course

The six stations

A PPE & Hand Hygiene · B Chairside Four-Handed · C Sterilisation · D School Screening · E Virtual Patient · F Emergency Drill. Each station carries its competency references and its step in the seven-step progression. The playable prototype and its stated limits are on the VR training page; one session is fully playable end to end, and the demo runs in a desktop/mobile browser view — a real WebXR session is not built.

How training is offered

Perilux is a demonstration and an offer. The offer page sets out the ask — expert review first, then a small pilot only under written agreement — and keeps the public facts about Unspoken Smiles in a separate, sourced box. Standards framing: the training is designed with reference to CODA, DANB, CDC and ADEA competencies — never "compliant with" or "accredited by" them. The licensure bridge (DANB CDA, California RDA, CDC guidance) is mapped, with primary sources, on the resources page; those credentials are the credentialing bodies', and this demo issues none.
